# Student quick start guide

A short guide for **high school volunteers** using the Down By The Border hours app to log service hours after events.

Staff looking for admin tools should use [STAFF_GUIDE.md](./STAFF_GUIDE.md) instead.

---

## Getting started

### 1. Create your account

1. Open the app link from your school or Down By The Border.
2. Click **Create an account**.
3. Enter your **first name**, **last name**, **email**, and a **password** (at least 8 characters).
4. Sign in if you’re not taken straight to the next step.

### 2. Complete your profile

The first time you sign in, you’ll be asked for:

- **Your phone number**
- **School** — start typing yours, or choose **Not applicable** if it doesn’t apply

You can add **parent or guardian** contact info later under **Profile** (some schools want that on file).

---

## Logging your hours

Do this **after each volunteer event** while the details are still fresh.

1. Tap **Log hours** in the top menu.
2. Fill in:
   - **Event name** — what you volunteered at (e.g. “Food drive”, “Field Day")
   - **Event date**
   - **Sign-in time** and **Sign-out time** — hours are calculated automatically
   - **Notes** (optional) — anything your school should know
3. Tap **Submit hours**.

You’ll see a confirmation message. Your hours show up on **Dashboard** and **My hours** right away — no waiting for approval.

---

## Viewing your hours

### Dashboard

- Your **total hours** so far
- **Recent activity** — your latest entries
- A button to **Log hours**

### My hours

- Full list of everything you’ve submitted
- Tap **Date** to sort oldest ↔ newest
- **Edit** or **Delete** only if the entry is less than **24 hours** old (see below)

---

## Fixing a mistake

Made a typo or wrong time?

- You have **24 hours** after submitting to **Edit** or **Delete** a log yourself.
- After 24 hours, the entry is **locked** for you — ask Down By The Border staff to fix it. They can update it from their admin tools.

---

## Your profile

Open **Profile** anytime to update:

- Name, phone, school  
- Parent or guardian name, phone, and email (if your school needs it)

Your **email** is your login and can’t be changed here. If you need a new email, talk to staff.

---

## Letters for school

Need proof of volunteer hours for a counselor or service requirement?

**You don’t download the official letter yourself** — staff generate a PDF from your record (with the Down By The Border logo and signature).

1. Make sure your hours are logged correctly in the app.
2. Ask a Down By The Border staff member to download your **service-hour letter**.
3. They can include all your hours or a specific date range.

Keep logging hours after every event so your record stays up to date.

---

## Tips

- Log hours **the same day** as the event when you can.
- Use a clear **event name** so schools recognize the activity.
- Double-check **sign-in** and **sign-out** times before you submit.
- Use a password you’ll remember, or save it in your phone’s password manager.

---

## Quick reference

| I want to… | Go to… |
|------------|--------|
| Log new hours | **Log hours** |
| See everything I’ve submitted | **My hours** |
| See my total | **Dashboard** |
| Fix a recent entry | **My hours** → **Edit** (within 24 hours) |
| Update phone, school, parent info | **Profile** |
| Official letter for school | Ask staff (see above) |

---

## Need help?

- **Can’t sign in** — try password reset on the login page, or ask staff.
- **Hours look wrong** — edit within 24 hours, or ask staff to fix older entries.
- **Don’t see my account** — confirm you signed up with the right email.

For staff and technical issues, your contact at Down By The Border can help.
